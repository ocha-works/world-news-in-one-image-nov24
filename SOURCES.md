## 1. Korea — Russian jets spark a false alarm off Korea (lead)

- Yonhap News Agency, "Russian warplanes enter air defense zone:
  S. Korean military", 09:24, 24 November 2026 — several Russian
  warplanes briefly entered South Korea's air defence identification
  zone (KADIZ) over the East Sea early Tuesday, approaching Ulleung
  Island; the Air Force scrambled fighter jets; the planes exited
  without violating South Korean airspace.
- Maeil Business Newspaper (MK), 24 November 2026 — the Ministry of
  the Interior and Safety issued an alert at 7:52 a.m. saying an
  aircraft believed to be Russian had violated territorial airspace,
  lifted it three minutes later; North Gyeongsang Province then sent
  a message at 7:58 a.m. wrongly saying a presumed North Korean
  aircraft had left the danger zone, a training phrase sent by
  mistake; corrected at 8:39 a.m.
- SBS News, 24 November 2026 11:21+09:00, and Chosun Biz, 24 November
  2026 — same sequence: Russian military aircraft approached Ulleung
  Island, the alert was issued, the lift message mistakenly used a
  routine training phrase about a presumed North Korean aircraft.

  published in Dawn, 24 November 2026 — Bangladesh will arrest and
